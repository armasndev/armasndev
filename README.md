### 💡 Engineering Philosophy & Approach

* **Invisible & Frictionless Usability:** Software should execute reliably without forcing the user to think. I prioritize low latency, rock-solid consistency, and intuitive design over clever abstractions.
* **Taming Non-Deterministic AI:** LLMs are powerful world-knowledge engines, but inherently chaotic, expensive, and unpredictable in production. My work centers on building the surrounding machinery—grounding, evaluation, tooling, and guardrails—that transforms probabilistic models into reliable enterprise tools.
* **Balanced Engineering Discipline:** I fight over-engineering, but respect the line against under-engineering. In an era where AI dramatically speeds up feature development, the core challenge is applying rigor and discipline so every addition remains genuinely useful, maintainable, and readable.
* **Deep Understanding First:** I don't use tools I don't understand. Fundamentals dictate *how* things work, but engineering experience dictates *when* and *why* to use them.
